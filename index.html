Frequently asked questions

What YouTube URL formats are supported?

All formats work — youtube.com/watch?v=..., youtu.be/..., youtube.com/shorts/..., and even just the video ID directly.

Is this tool really free?

Yes, 100% free. No login, no signup, no limit. YouTube thumbnails are publicly accessible images.

What is the best thumbnail size to download?

Maxres (1280×720) is the highest quality. Use it for presentations, designs, or video editing. Not all videos have maxres — in that case HQ (480×360) is the best available.

Can I use downloaded thumbnails commercially?

Thumbnails belong to the video creators. For personal/reference use it's fine. For commercial use, get permission from the content creator.

Why does maxres show a blank image sometimes?

Not all YouTube videos have a maxres thumbnail — older or less popular videos may only have HQ or SD. Use HQ in that case.
